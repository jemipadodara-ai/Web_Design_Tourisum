1. Kaziranga National Park - A Wildlife Haven

Kaziranga National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, is renowned for its population of the Indian one-horned rhinoceros. The park also hosts tigers, elephants, wild water buffalo, and various bird species.

  • Jeep Safaris: Explore the park's diverse flora and fauna.
  • Elephant Rides: Get a closer look at the wildlife.
  • Bird Watching: Spot a variety of bird species.

2. Majuli - The World's Largest River Island

Majuli, situated on the Brahmaputra River, is the world's largest river island and a cultural hub of Assam. It is known for its vibrant festivals and scenic beauty.

  • Sattras: Ancient Vaishnavite monasteries.
  • Festivals: Experience Raas Leela and Ali-Ai-Ligang.
  • Handicrafts: Mask-making, pottery, and weaving.

Additional Information about Assam:

  1. Best Season: November to April.
  2. Culture: Assamese traditions, folk music, dance,and Bihu.
  3. Activities: Wildlife safaris, river cruises, and traditional cuisine.
