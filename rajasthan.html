1. Jaipur - The Pink City

Known as the "Pink City," Jaipur is the capital of Rajasthan and a treasure trove of royal heritage. Its iconic architecture, bustling bazaars, and rich history make it a must-visit destination.

  • Amber Fort: Known for magnificent architecture and elephant rides.
  • Hawa Mahal: A stunning five-story palace with 953 small windows.
  • City Palace: A blend of Mughal and Rajasthani architecture.

2. Udaipur - The City of Lakes

Famous for its picturesque lakes and palaces, Udaipur is often referred to as the "Venice of the East". The city's romantic ambiance makes it a favorite among travelers.

  • Lake Pichola: A beautiful artificial lake surrounded by palaces.
  • Jag Mandir: A stunning island palace known for serene surroundings.

Additional Information about Rajasthan

  • Best Season: October to March (Pleasant weather and vibrant festivals).
  • Culture: A rich blend of Rajput traditions, folk music, and dance.
  • Cuisine: Don't miss Dal Baati Churma, Gatte ki Sabzi, and Laal Maas.
  • Shopping: Famous for textiles, jewelry, pottery, and handicrafts.
