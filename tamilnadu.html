1. Chennai - Gateway to the South

Chennai, the capital of Tamil Nadu, is a vibrant city that perfectly blends traditional South Indian culture with modern urban life. It is famous for its historical landmarks and the iconic Marina Beach.

  • Marina Beach: One of the longest urban beaches in the world.
  • Kapaleeshwarar Temple: A stunning example of Dravidian architecture.
  • San Thome Basilica: A historic church built over the tomb of an apostle.

2. Madurai - The Temple City

Madurai is one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world and is the cultural soul of Tamil Nadu. It is home to the magnificent Meenakshi Amman Temple.

  • Meenakshi Temple: Famous for its towering gopurams and intricate carvings.
  • Thirumalai Nayakkar Mahal: A 17th-century palace showcasing Indo-Saracenic style.
  • Night Ceremony: Witness the rhythmic prayers and processions at the temple.

Additional Information about Tamil Nadu

  • Best Season: November to February when the weather is cool and pleasant.
  • Culture: Rich in classical arts like Bharatanatyam and Carnatic music.
  • Cuisine: Famous for Idli, Dosa, Sambhar, and the unique Chettinad flavors.
  • Shopping: Renowned for Kanchipuram silk sarees and bronze icons.
