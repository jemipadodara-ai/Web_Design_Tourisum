1. Srinagar - The Summer Capital

Known as the "Jewel of Kashmir," Srinagar is world-famous for its canals, houseboats, and Mughal gardens.

Culture: A blend of Sufi traditions and Kashmiri craftsmanship, famous for Wazwan meals and Pashmina weaving.

  • Shikara ride on Dal Lake
  • Staying in a traditional wooden Houseboat
  • Tulip Garden (Asia's largest)

Best Season: April to October.

2. Gulmarg - The Meadow of Flowers

A popular hill station and skiing destination known for stunning meadows and snow-capped peaks.

  • Skiing and snowboarding in winter
  • Gondola ride to Apharwat Peak
  • World's highest golf courses

Best Season: Dec to Feb (sports); May to Sept (sightseeing).

Traveler Essentials

  • Local Cuisine: Try Rogan Josh, Yakhni, Gustaba, and Kahwa tea.
  • Shopping: Famous for Pashmina shawls, carpets, saffron, and dry fruits.
  • Transport: Well-connected by road, rail, and Srinagar International Airport.
